# Personal Publishing Site

The public home for Travis Fischer's identity and long-form work. It exists to create visual and technical intrigue, then lead visitors into articles.

## Language

**Landing experience**: The identity-first root page composed of a computational wordmark, an author letter, and the article index. _Avoid_: Home document, Notion page

**Signature scene**: A novel interactive visual that establishes technical craft and creates intrigue at the start of the landing experience. _Avoid_: Hero animation, animated background

**Computational wordmark**: The oversized interactive typography that serves as the signature scene and the site's primary identity mark. _Avoid_: Logo animation, shader demo

**Signature phrase**: “YOOOOOOOO!!!!” — exactly eight O’s — the personal expression given primary visual emphasis by the computational wordmark. _Avoid_: Headline carousel, tagline

**Descriptor**: A secondary phrase identifying an area of Travis's work, such as “Open Source BS” or “AI Engineering BS.” _Avoid_: Hero phrase, carousel slide

**Responsive instrument**: A signature scene that remains complete without interaction but responds expressively to pointer or touch input. _Avoid_: Ambient background, required interaction, minigame

**Author letter**: A short personal interlude between the computational wordmark and the article index that frames the work in the author's voice. _Avoid_: About excerpt, biography section

**Article index**: The primary collection of published articles that follows the identity introduction. _Avoid_: Blog gallery, Notion database

**Article**: A long-form published work containing prose and potentially code, images, video, or other explanatory media. _Avoid_: Database page, post card

**Editorial content**: Author-managed prose and media whose meaning is independent of the site's presentation. _Avoid_: Notion content, Notion blocks

**Index artwork**: A promotional representation of an article within the article index that may receive a brand-specific visual treatment. _Avoid_: Thumbnail, Notion cover

**Article media**: Source-faithful images and video embedded within an article whose original visual information must be preserved. _Avoid_: Index artwork, marketing image

**Presentation shell**: The identity, navigation, composition, and visual system surrounding editorial content. _Avoid_: Notion shell, starter-kit theme
